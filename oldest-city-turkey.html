Çatalhöyük is the oldest known proto-city settlement in Turkey, dating back to around 7500 BC (roughly 9,500 years ago). Wikipedia +11

Located on the Konya Plain in southern Anatolia, this UNESCO World Heritage Site was inhabited through the Neolithic and into the Chalcolithic period until around 5600–5700 BC. According to GoTürkiye1, it housed between 5,000 and 10,000 people at its peak and is often cited as one of the world's earliest urban settlements. GoTürkiye +31

Key Features

  • Streetless layout: Residents built closely packed mudbrick homes and moved across rooftops, entering houses through roof openings via ladders.
  • Culture and art: Excavations have uncovered wall paintings, reliefs, figurines, and burials beneath house floors.
