Turkey features 22 UNESCO World Heritage Sites and hundreds of archaeological and architectural landmarks spanning prehistoric, classical, Byzantine, and Ottoman eras. Key historical sites across the country include: Wikipedia +21

Prehistoric & Ancient Cities

  • Göbeklitepe (Şanlıurfa): Pre-Pottery Neolithic site from around 9,600 BCE featuring carved T-shaped limestone pillars.
  • Ephesus Archaeological Site (İzmir): Major Greco-Roman city known for the Library of Celsus, Temple of Hadrian, and Terrace Houses.
  • Hierapolis & Pamukkale (Denizli): Ancient spa city with Roman ruins alongside white travertine terraces.
  • Troy (Çanakkale) & Hattusha (Çorum): Archaeological sites of the legendary Trojan city and the Hittite Empire capital.
  • Aphrodisias (Aydın) & Pergamon (İzmir): Classical centers famed for marble sculptures, temples, and acropolis ruins. Fez Travel +41

Istanbul Landmarks

  • Hagia Sophia Grand Mosque: 6th-century Byzantine dome structure later converted into an imperial mosque, showcasing both Christian mosaics and Islamic calligraphy.
  • Topkapı Palace & The Blue Mosque: Centerpieces of Ottoman imperial history and architecture in the historic peninsula.
  • Basilica Cistern & Rumeli Fortress: Subterranean Byzantine water reservoir and 15th-century Bosphorus fortress. GoTürkiye +41

Cappadocia, Anatolia & Coasts

  • Göreme Open Air Museum & Derinkuyu Underground City (Nevşehir): Rock-cut churches, cave dwellings, and multi-level subterranean settlements.
